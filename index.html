About Us

Who we are, what we do, and what we’ve accomplished.

Classroom Care is a student-led nonprofit advancing educational equity through visits to elementary schools that include school supply and toy donations, book readings, and hands-on crafts. Over the years, we have partnered with five schools, raised more than $6,000, and brought smiles to hundreds of students.
